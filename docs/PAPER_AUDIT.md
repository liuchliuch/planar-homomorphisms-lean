# Paper-to-Lean audit

Paper: Chenghua Liu and Boning Meng, *A Dichotomy for Planar Graph
Homomorphisms with Nonnegative Weights*, 92 pages. The PDF SHA-256 is
`366b92c0dfafc076a43751b04cae1296562d3b87ec97a9b27d8a25085dc407f5`.
The numbered headings were extracted from that PDF: 44 body proof statements
and 14 Appendix A proof statements. Definitions and remarks are not counted
as proved theorems.

This review compared all 58 numbered statements with their Lean interfaces,
read the underlying graph, structural, encoding, machine, and reduction
definitions, and followed selected proof chains through the closed foundations.
It is not a manual line-by-line review of all 4,125 production modules.
Kernel compilation and transitive axiom auditing are separate evidence:
see [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

No substantive weakened final classification or unproved computational
foundation was identified in this review. The closed Corollary 12.2 interface discharges the proved Potts foundation
without an extra premise. Abstract closure interfaces in Chapters 3–4 are genuine
paper hypotheses and are separately instantiated for concrete source languages.

Lemma 4.5 uses an analytic exponential-dual completion proof rather than
reproducing every step of the paper's primal entropy argument. Its continuation
and gradient facts are proved before use. The fixed-real output model and the
finite surface-embedding boundary require the qualifications in
[SEMANTICS.md](SEMANTICS.md). The appendix does not prove a complete dichotomy for
all signed matrices.

The machine-readable [ledger](paper-ledger.json) gives exact declaration names
and local source locations. The compiled inventory prints full elaborated
signatures and allowed axiom dependencies. It does not replace the semantic
review below. All 100 entry types also have fixed reviewed statement/solution
contracts with added-premise rejection controls; three primary propositions were
written independently. See [COMPARATOR.md](COMPARATOR.md) for the distinction
between source-derived reviewed contracts and independent specifications.
